Unit 8: Probability (17 Questions)

1. Write probability words on this line to make it into a likelihood scale.
Answer: Impossible, Unlikely, Even chance, Likely, Certain
2. Cards numbered 1 to 10 are placed in a hat. Bella takes a card. Describe the likelihood that she picks: the number 7.
Answer: Unlikely
3. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking an even number.
Answer: Even chance
4. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking a number greater than 6.
Answer: Unlikely (Numbers 7, 8, 9, 10 are 4 out of 10)
5. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking the number 12.
Answer: Impossible
6. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking a number less than 3.
Answer: Unlikely (Numbers 1, 2)
7. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking a number between 2 and 9.
Answer: Likely (Numbers 3 to 8)
8. For cards 1 to 10, describe the likelihood of picking a number less than 12.
Answer: Certain
9. On an 8-section spinner (sections numbered 1, 2, 1, 4, 1, 2, 3, 1), which numbers are equally likely?
Answer: Number 2 and Number 3 (both appear 2 times)
10. On the same 8-section spinner, which number is most likely to land on?
Answer: Number 1 (appears 4 times)
11. On the same 8-section spinner, is it more likely to land on a 2 or a 3?
Answer: Equally likely (both appear twice)
12. Simulation: If there is an even chance of a baby elephant being male or female, use a coin to simulate 10 births. How many male/female could be born?
Answer: Experimental results vary (approx. 5 male, 5 female).
13. Three girls played 'hit the target'. Each had 10 goes. Zina hit 4 times. Roz hit 60% of the time. Jodi hit the target 2/5 of the time. Who hit the target the most?
Answer: Roz (60% = 6 out of 10, Jodi = 4 out of 10, Zina = 4 out of 10. Roz hit the most).
14. What is the probability of rolling an even number on a standard 6-sided die?
Answer: Even chance (3 out of 6)
15. What is the probability of rolling a number greater than 6 on a standard 6-sided die?
Answer: Impossible (0)
16. If you flip a coin 50 times, roughly how many times would you expect to get heads?
Answer: Around 25 times (Even chance)
17. A bag contains 3 red marbles, 2 blue marbles, and 5 green marbles. What is the likelihood of picking a green marble?
Answer: Even chance (5 out of 10)

Unit 9: Advanced Fractions (Like, Unlike, Mixed Numbers & Problem Solving)

1. **Like Fractions Example:** Calculate 5/8 - 1/8.
Answer: 4/8 (simplifies to 1/2)
2. **Unlike Fractions Problem:** Calculate 5/12 + 1/6.
Answer: 7/12 (Convert 1/6 to 2/12, then add 5/12 + 2/12)
3. **Unlike Fractions Problem:** Calculate 2/3 - 1/6.
Answer: 3/6 (simplifies to 1/2)
4. **Mixed Numbers Problem (Detailed Steps):** Calculate 2 1/5 + 1 3/5.
Answer: 3 4/5 (Convert to improper: 11/5 + 8/5 = 19/5 = 3 4/5)
5. **Garden Fraction Problem:** In a diagram of a garden, 5/8 is planted with roses, 1/4 is planted with shrubs. What fraction of the garden is planted with vegetables?
Answer: 1/8 (5/8 + 2/8 = 7/8; whole is 8/8 - 7/8 = 1/8)
6. **Addition Debate Problem:** Ben and Bashir work out 4/15 + 3/5. Ben says the answer is 13/15. Bashir says the answer is 7/20. Who do you agree with? Explain.
Answer: Agree with Ben (13/15). Convert 3/5 to 9/15, then add 4/15 + 9/15 = 13/15. Bashir incorrectly added both tops and bottoms.
