Short Questions & Problem Solving (Complete Practice)

1. List all the composite numbers between 10 and 20.
Answer: 12, 14, 15, 16, 18
2. List all the prime numbers between 10 and 30.
Answer: 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29
3. Write down all the square numbers that are less than 100.
Answer: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81
4. Find the mode and median of the set: 13, 17, 24, 19, 26, 17, 25, 21, 12.
Answer: Mode = 17, Median = 19
5. Calculate the fraction addition: 5/12 + 1/6 = ?
Answer: 7/12
6. Calculate the mixed number addition: 2 1/5 + 1 3/5 = ?
Answer: 3 4/5 (or 19/5)
7. Find the difference between 78.43 and 48.75.
Answer: 29.68
