Multiple Choice Questions (Complete Practice)

1. Which of the following is a prime number?
  • A) 21
  • B) 11
  • C) 18
  • D) 27
Answer: B) 11
2. Which number from the set {12, 7, 9, 18, 23} is both even and composite?
  • A) 7
  • B) 9
  • C) 12
  • D) 23
Answer: C) 12
3. What is the simplest form of the fraction 12/16?
  • A) 1/2
  • B) 3/4
  • C) 2/3
  • D) 4/5
Answer: B) 3/4
4. If you draw a card numbered 1 to 10, what is the likelihood of picking an even number?
  • A) Impossible
  • B) Even chance
  • C) Certain
Answer: B) Even chance
5. Which fraction is equal to 20%?
  • A) 1/2
  • B) 1/20
  • C) 1/5
  • D) 3/15
Answer: C) 1/5
